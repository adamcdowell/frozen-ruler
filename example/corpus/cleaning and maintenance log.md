# Cleaning and maintenance log

Standing schedule and running log. Backflush with water daily, with detergent
weekly. Wipe and purge the steam wand after every use — dried milk is the
fastest way to ruin a wand tip.

## Schedule

- Daily: water backflush, wand purge, drip tray
- Weekly: detergent backflush, soak portafilter + basket, brush the group
- Monthly: check shower screen, inspect group gasket
- Quarterly: descale (skip if on remineralized RO — see
  [[water chemistry for espresso]])
