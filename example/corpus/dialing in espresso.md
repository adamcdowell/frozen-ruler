# Dialing in espresso

The core recipe triangle is dose, yield, and time. Start at an 18g dose, a 36g
yield (a 1:2 ratio), and adjust grind until the shot runs 25–30 seconds.

A shot that runs fast and tastes sour is under-extracted: grind finer or slow
the flow. A shot that runs long and tastes bitter or hollow is over-extracted:
grind coarser. Change ONE variable at a time — grind first, dose second, ratio
last — and log every change, or you are guessing, not dialing.

Grind consistency is upstream of everything here; see [[grinder burr types]].
If shots vary wildly at the same setting, suspect [[channeling]] before blaming
the beans.

## Reference recipe

- Dose: 18g in
- Yield: 36g out
- Time: 25–30s from first drip
- Temperature: 93°C for medium roasts, 90–91°C for light roasts
