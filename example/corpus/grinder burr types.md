# Grinder burr types

Flat burrs and conical burrs produce different particle distributions. Flat
burrs cut a tighter, more unimodal distribution — clarity and separation in the
cup, at the cost of more retention and heat at high volume. Conical burrs
produce a wider bimodal distribution — more body and forgiveness, slightly less
clarity.

For espresso, alignment matters more than geometry: a misaligned flat burr set
throws fines that behave like a much cheaper grinder. Particle consistency is
the single biggest equipment lever on repeatability — an inconsistent grind
shows up directly as shot-to-shot variance when [[dialing in espresso]].

Burr seasoning is real but overstated: expect the first few kilos to shift the
grind setting slightly, then stability.
